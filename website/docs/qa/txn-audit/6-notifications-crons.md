# Transaction audit 6: notifications and scheduled jobs

Scope: every `createNotification` call site in `src`, every cron in `vercel.json` plus `/api/stripe-connect/process-pending`, and the cross-check of bell links against real routes. Read-only audit; no source changed. All paths relative to `website/`.

Mechanism notes that frame everything below:

- `src/lib/notifications.ts` is the single insert path. It swallows all errors (logged via `console.error`), takes no idempotency key, and the `notifications` table (migration `supabase/migrations/007_notifications_and_placement_flags.sql`) has no unique constraint. Bells therefore have NO dedup anywhere; only emails do (`email_events.idempotency_key` in `src/lib/email/send.ts`).
- The Stripe webhook (`src/app/api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts:86-131`) claims each `event.id` in `stripe_webhook_events` before processing and releases the claim on any 5xx. Redelivery of the same event id is a no-op, but a branch that bells and then 500s re-runs in full on Stripe's retry, and the bell fires again.
- The bell dropdown (`src/components/Header.tsx:648-700`) renders `link` through Next `<Link>`, with a portal-aware fallback when `link` is empty. Absolute URLs work but hard-navigate to whatever `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` was at insert time.

## Transaction moments with no bell

Money and transaction moments enumerated from first principles, with what exists today:

1. **Paid-loan monthly payment failed** (venue card declines): nothing at all. `handleInvoicePaymentFailed` (`src/lib/placements/paid-loan-billing.ts:336-371`) flips the row to `past_due`/`paused` and its own doc comment says "notify both parties", but no bell and no email is ever sent. The artist's income stops silently; the venue is never told to fix the card. (F2)
2. **Paid-loan subscription cancelled** (`customer.subscription.deleted`): status flip only, no bell or email to the artist that monthly payments have ended (`paid-loan-billing.ts:374-401`). (F2)
3. **Curation subscription payment failed/paused**: `handleCurationInvoiceFailed` (`src/lib/curation/billing.ts:146-165`) updates status only; the venue is not told their managed curation stopped being paid for. (F2)
4. **Payout failed** (`payout.failed`): email only (`webhooks/stripe/route.ts:1798-1820`), no bell, although this is the payout event that needs artist action. Its twin `payout.paid` has both. (F5)
5. **Transfer retries exhausted**: admin alert only (`src/lib/stripe-connect.ts alertExhaustedPayout`); the artist whose money is stuck sees nothing. Defensible (operator-first), but worth an artist-facing signal once an operator confirms.
6. **Chargeback / dispute opened or resolved** (`charge.dispute.*`): no webhook branch at all, so no bell, no email, no admin alert, no order-state change. `route.test.ts:1728` pins the adjacent `charge.refunded` fall-through as a 200. (F3)
7. **Out-of-band Stripe refund** (`charge.refunded` issued from the Stripe dashboard): unhandled; order stays confirmed, nobody notified. (F3)
8. **Refund rejected**: email to the requester only (`src/app/api/refunds/process/route.ts:120-165`); no bell, while both approve-side bells exist. (F4)
9. **Platform subscription payment failed / trial ending / started / upgraded / cancelled**: email only. Acceptable for account lifecycle, but "your subscription payment failed" is arguably a transaction alert.
10. **Order shipped / delivered / delivery auto-confirmed**: buyer gets emails only; account-holding buyers get no bell, and the 7-day auto-confirm writes an event with no notice to anyone. Tolerable because most buyers are guests.
11. **Offer expired**: nothing anywhere. `expires_at` is only stamped `expired` lazily if the buyer attempts checkout on a stale offer (`src/app/api/offers/[id]/checkout/route.ts:97-102`); no sweep, no notification to either side.
12. **Artist Stripe KYC required** (`ArtistStripeKycNeeded` email exists in the webhook): email only, no bell.
13. **Offer paid**: covered for the artist via the shared `sale` bell (`confirmations.ts:177`); the paying venue's own success is shown by redirect and email receipt. No gap.
