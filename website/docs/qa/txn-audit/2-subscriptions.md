# Transaction audit 2: subscriptions and recurring billing

Forensic pass over every recurring-billing product: the artist SaaS plan (`src/app/api/subscribe/route.ts`, `src/app/api/subscribe/portal/route.ts`), paid-loan monthly billing (`src/app/api/placements/[id]/payment/setup/route.ts`, `src/lib/placements/paid-loan-billing.ts`), and the managed curation retainer (`src/app/api/curation/route.ts`, `src/lib/curation/billing.ts`), together with every `customer.subscription.*` and `invoice.*` branch of the Stripe webhook (`src/app/api/webhooks/stripe/route.ts`), the GATING_V1 delisting filter (`src/lib/db/merged-data.ts`), the email pipeline (`src/lib/email/send.ts`, `src/lib/email/categories.ts`, `src/emails/registry.ts`) and the finance reconciler (`src/lib/finance/reconcile.ts`). Motivating incident: the owner's own artist subscription was auto-cancelled by Stripe with no warning email ever received, he was silently removed from /browse, and his invoices arrived on three different monthly anchor dates, which is the signature of concurrent subscriptions having existed.

Date: 2026-08-28. Branch: claude/wallplace-remediation-finish-42e4c1. No source code was modified.

Summary of the incident against current code: the three anchor dates are explained by `/api/subscribe` creating a brand-new Stripe subscription (new anchor) on every plan change or reactivation, with three separate ways for the old subscription to survive alongside the new one (findings 1 and 5). The silent delisting is fully reproduced by current code: GATING_V1 drops any artist whose `subscription_status` is not active/trialing, the status flips to `past_due` on the first failed invoice before the dunning email is even dispatched, and no email, banner or notification anywhere mentions /browse visibility (finding 2). The "no warning" half is partly fixed (the dunning email now exists and is unsuppressible) but the two warnings that should arrive before any charge fails, card-expiring and renewal-upcoming, still do not exist, and the trial-ending warning is classified as suppressible marketing (findings 3 and 4).

## The five specific questions

### (a) Does invoice.payment_failed reach the artist as an email, and can email category gating suppress it?

Yes it reaches them, and no, category gating cannot suppress it. The branch at webhook route.ts:1584-1635 sends `subscription_payment_failed` on every attempt, keyed `payment_failed:{invoiceId}:{attempt_count}` so each Stripe retry produces one fresh email. Its category is `orders_and_payouts`, which carries `criticalAlwaysSend: true` (categories.ts:34): the suppression check (send.ts:90), the preference and vacation checks (send.ts:110-127) and the throttle (throttleCount 0) are all bypassed, so no user toggle, unsubscribe, vacation mode or suppression row can stop it. What CAN silently stop it: a missing RESEND_API_KEY (hard failure in prod, soft skip elsewhere, send.ts:175-190), EMAIL_DRY_RUN, the artist profile missing a user_id or auth email (route.ts:1603-1605), a previously failed email_events row under the same key (finding 16), and any email exception, which is caught and logged without failing the webhook (:1631-1633). The suppressible one is the WARNING BEFORE the failure: `subscription_trial_ending` is category "promotions", which is preference-gated, defaults to off once a preferences row exists, is throttled 2 per 720 h, and is blocked by marketing suppressions (finding 3). The other two pre-failure warnings, card-expiring and renewal-upcoming, do not exist at all (finding 4).

### (b) Is the artist EVER told that lapsing delists them from /browse, before or after it happens?

No, never, on any surface. Delisting is `getAllArtists` filtering to active/trialing under GATING_V1 (merged-data.ts:6, 39-42; flag ships on in prod), consumed by /api/browse-artists. `past_due` is outside the active set, so the artist disappears from /browse at the first failed invoice, and the webhook writes past_due (route.ts:1589) before dispatching the dunning email (:1612), so the removal precedes the first notice of any problem. The dunning email says "Update your card before then to avoid losing access" (already lost), the cancellation email says "You'll keep full access until <date>" (already ended), the billing page banners mention only "accessing your portal" and reactivation (billing/page.tsx:380-391), and no bell notification for any subscription event exists (the three createNotification calls in the webhook are paid-loan start and payouts). No email template in src/emails mentions browse, listing, visibility or delisting (grep confirms zero hits). The artist's own profile URL keeps working (merged-data.ts:48-58), which makes the disappearance harder to notice, not easier.

### (c) Can /api/subscribe create a second concurrent subscription for an artist who already has one?

Yes, by at least three paths. (1) Re-subscribe while past_due: the existing-subscription lookup is gated on the DB reading active/trialing, so `cancel_previous` is empty and the past_due subscription survives alongside the new one, still invoicing on its own anchor; the billing page's past_due state presents the plan picker that triggers exactly this (finding 1). (2) Double submit: no Stripe idempotency key on the Checkout session, so two clicks give two completable sessions; both complete, each created-event cancels only the OLD subscription named in its own metadata, and neither cancels the other (finding 5). (3) Silent cancel failure: the `stripe.subscriptions.cancel(cancel_previous)` error is swallowed with no retry or alert, and `subscriptions.list` is limit 1 with its own errors swallowed, so an upgrade can leave the previous subscription billing (webhook route.ts:1297-1306; subscribe/route.ts:71-80). There is no webhook-side detector for a second live SaaS subscription and no SaaS subscription ledger, only one overwriting column, so a concurrent pair is invisible in-app; its only symptom is doubled invoices and renewal receipts. Every plan change also creates a NEW subscription with a NEW anchor date by design, which together with these paths reproduces the owner's three anchor dates.

### (d) Same parallel-billing question for paid-loan (one placement, two subscriptions) and curation

Paid loan: substantially guarded, one real gap. The setup route 409s when a live ledger row or the placements mirror exists, and carries a 1 h Stripe idempotency key, but a second session minted across the window boundary (or after a fee edit changes the key) is completable for 24 h; when it completes, the partial unique index refuses the second ledger row (duplicate_live_billing) but the webhook acknowledges with 200 and leaves the second Stripe subscription LIVE and billing the venue, with only a console.error, no admin alert and no cancellation (finding 6). Curation: wide open. Each POST /api/curation with a managed tier creates an independent row and checkout session, anonymously, with no idempotency key and no dedup by contact or venue; every completed session becomes its own legitimate in_progress subscription, so a double submit (or resubmission after an abandoned checkout) yields two live retainers billing the same venue with nothing anywhere to notice (finding 10).

### (e) Does any subscription write match rows by stripe_customer_id in a way that could hit the wrong profile?

Four SaaS branches write artist_profiles by `stripe_customer_id` alone: created/updated (:1282-1294), deleted (:1544-1547), payment_failed (:1589-1592) and invoice.paid recovery (:1710-1714). No migration adds a unique constraint on that column, so if two profiles ever shared a customer id (nothing in code creates this today, but nothing in the schema forbids it) the status writes would hit both, and the invoice.paid path would do the opposite: its `.single()` errors on 2+ rows, the error is discarded, and recovery plus receipt silently skip. Cross-product contamination is currently prevented by construction rather than by checks in most branches: paid-loan and curation checkouts use `customer_email` so Stripe mints a fresh customer per completed session, meaning their invoices' customer ids can never equal an artist's SaaS customer id; only created/updated additionally carries the D15 metadata-kind guard, while payment_failed, deleted and invoice.paid rely purely on that disjointness, which the D15 comment itself describes as "one refactor away" from breaking (e.g. anyone reusing an existing customer for paid-loan setup would make a venue's failed loan invoice mark an artist-who-is-also-that-venue past_due and delist them). The realistic wrong-profile bug today is the inverse miss: the /api/subscribe get-or-create race (finding 9) can leave the checkout session pointing at a customer id no profile stores, after which every subscription write matches zero rows and a paying artist stays delisted with no reconciler to catch it (finding 17).
