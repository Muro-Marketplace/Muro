Auditor: purchase money-in expert, forensic transaction audit, 2026-08-28.
Scope: every way money comes IN for artwork. Cart checkout (ship, collection, collect_venue), purchase-offer checkout, paid-loan monthly billing. Curation payments and SaaS artist subscriptions share the webhook and are noted as adjacent rows only; they belong to the subscriptions auditor.

Context that shapes several verdicts: all three session creators pin `payment_method_types: ["card"]` (checkout:696, offer-checkout:166, placements payment/setup:122). Card settles synchronously inside Checkout, so today `checkout.session.completed` arrives already paid and the async events cannot fire. Several MISSING verdicts below are therefore latent, and they detonate the day any delayed payment method is enabled.

## Invariants checked

**1. Money conservation per fulfilment mode and cart shape.**
Cart (ship): assertLegsReconcile (legs:251-265, called webhook:794) enforces venue + platform fee + sum(leg nets) = amount_total exactly, with per-line venue-cut rounding and per-artist fee rounding absorbed into the platform fee by reconcilePlatformFee (legs:228-248), which warns when the residual exceeds max(1, legs) pence. Shipping flows fee-free to the artist who posts (allocateShipping, legs:65-103, exact to the penny with a deterministic remainder anchor). HOLDS, pinned by legs.test.ts ("splits to the penny", shipping attribution suite) and route.test.ts:1148. Collection and collect_venue: shipping total is 0 and the named map scales down to 0 (legs:78-82); transfers go immediate (webhook:2000-2010). HOLDS. Caveat: a line with an empty artistSlug contributes to total but to no leg, so its full value lands in the platform fee with only a warn; unreachable from the product UI (every add-to-cart sets artistSlug; collection lines too, browse/collections page:128) but reachable by hand-rolled request. Offer: fee + net = amount charged by remainder construction (offer-checkout:150-152), venue 0. HOLDS (offer tests 701, 152). Paid-loan: share = recorded monthly fee x (1 - fee%), NOT reconciled against invoice.amount_paid. BROKEN latent (F6).

**2. Idempotency under Stripe redelivery, every branch.**
Layer 1: global event-id claim with release-on-5xx (webhook:86-125). Layer 2, cart: deterministic WS- order id (order-id:17-20), F30 payment-intent pre-check (webhook:873-888), 23505 classified duplicate vs collision (order-id:34-49), duplicate paths re-run scheduleOrderLegs which is a no-op for existing legs via the (order_id, recipient_user_id) unique index (connect:64-75). Layer 2, offer: same OFR- id derivation plus classify; flip and emails re-run harmlessly (email idempotency keys, event keyed on payment intent). Layer 2, paid-loan: upsert on subscription id, invoice-level transfer pre-check (paid-loan:306-315), newlyLinked gates the bell. Stock decrement is only reached on the fresh-insert path, so it cannot double-run across redeliveries. Emails are all idempotency-keyed; bells are not keyed but sit on first-processing paths. HOLDS, with one asymmetry: F1 means the failure mode is not "processed twice" but "never retried at all".

**3. Partial-failure atomicity.**
Order-insert-first everywhere (the E6 lesson). After the cart insert: decrement, enrichment, confirmations and venue notices are best-effort by design (documented at webhook:1012-1027); transfers are last but recoverable via the D52.3 duplicate re-run and the zero-row reconcile sweep (connect:207-258). Gaps: a throw before the insert books nothing and is never retried (F1); the offer flip failure leaves a paid-but-accepted offer (F5); a PARTIAL leg failure is invisible to the sweep because it keys on "any transfer row exists", not per expected leg (F10). scheduleTransfer itself refuses to lose ledger rows (throws on insert failure, connect:64-84) but both cart and offer callers catch and log, so the throw's purpose is defeated at the call sites.

**4. QR attribution, work-level placement rate, collect_venue.**
The attribution token is HMAC-signed, venue+artist bound, 24h expiry, verified server-side and honoured only when the scanned artist is in the cart (qr-attribution-token.ts, checkout:156-169); bare venueSlug survives as a pre-enforcement fallback (checkout:170-172, owner-accepted transition) and enforcement fails closed with a 503 when the secret is missing (checkout:145-153, D39). Venue cut resolution: the sold work's own placement (artist_works.current_placement_id, filtered to the attributed venue and active status) wins per line, artist-level first-created placement is the deterministic fallback (webhook:693-737, legs:190-193, D9), no-active-placement computes 0 with a warn (D11). All pinned by tests (route.test.ts:392-546, legs.test.ts work-level suite). HOLDS for QR sales. BROKEN for collect_venue reached without QR context: venueSlug is empty so the venue share is 0 and the entire T9 block (venue email, bell, offer clearing) is skipped (F3). Self-purchase guard only binds when an Authorization header is present (checkout:180-208); guest self-purchase remains possible and is an accepted consequence of guest checkout, noted not counted.

**5. Quantity vs stock enforcement.**
Checkout rejects sold works (isWorkSold: available=false or tracked qty <= 0) and re-prices every line from the DB; the webhook decrements via the atomic RPC (migrations 085/120: serialised single UPDATE, NULL = unlimited untouched, clamp at 0, available flip at 0), restock on full refund only (refund-process:337-358). HOLDS for 1-of-1 works bought once. BROKEN for requested-quantity > stock (never compared, F4), for the mint-to-webhook window (no reservation, two paid buyers both book, F4), and for collections (no inventory effect at all, F9).

**Unhandled events judged dangerous** (the rest of the unhandled set is argued ignorable in the matrix): checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded for cart and offer sessions (F7, latent detonator), charge.refunded from the Stripe dashboard (F2), charge.dispute.created (F8), refund.failed (F2), and invoice.paid amount drift on paid-loan (F6, handled-but-wrong rather than unhandled).
